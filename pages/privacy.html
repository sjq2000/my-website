隐私与统计说明

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

计算、报告和分享卡

计算器在浏览器中运行。当前报告数据、语言偏好和部分访问状态保存在浏览器本地；清除浏览器数据可能使这些内容丢失。分享卡在本地生成，只包含预览中显示的内容。你主动下载、复制链接或通过系统分享发送时，才执行相应操作。身高分享链接不包含身体数值。

可选的 Google Analytics 统计

允许统计后，我们使用 Google Analytics 4 及 Cookie 衡量访问、页面类型、身高计算完成、报告购买按钮点击和分享操作，以改善网站。统计可能包含浏览器与设备信息、大致地区、来源渠道和用于区分浏览器的标识。我们不向该统计代码提供姓名、邮箱、生日、身体数值、基因坐标、表单内容或报告内容。

该代码只在你选择“允许统计”后加载；拒绝不影响计算和购买。你可以通过页脚“访问统计偏好”更改选择。撤回选择会停止此代码的后续统计，并移除本网站可访问的 GA Cookie，但不会自动删除已经处理的历史数据。广告个性化及 Google Signals 未启用。拒绝统计、拦截器及不同设备会影响数据完整性。

Google 可能在境外处理数据。查看 Google 如何使用合作网站的数据及其隐私权政策。

支付与其他第三方

付款跳转至 Stripe，由 Stripe 按其隐私权政策处理付款及订单信息。网站原有的访问量计数器由不蒜子提供，部分页面还加载外部字体或资源；这些请求可能使对应服务收到 IP 地址和浏览器请求信息。上面的 GA 统计选择仅控制新接入的 GA4，不控制原有第三方资源。

人工分析与联系

如果你选择通过邮箱提交资料，收件人将收到你主动发送的内容。有关这些说明或数据问题，请联系 songjueqing@gmail.com。

Privacy & analytics

Updated: 25 November 2026

Calculations, reports and share cards

Calculators run in your browser. Current report data, language preferences and some access state are stored locally; clearing browser data may remove them. Share cards are generated locally and contain only the previewed details. Downloading, copying a link or sharing requires your action. Height share links contain no body measurements.

Optional Google Analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics 4 and cookies measure visits, page categories, completed height calculations, report purchase-button clicks and sharing actions. Data may include browser and device details, approximate region, traffic sources and browser identifiers. We do not provide names, email addresses, birth dates, body measurements, genetic coordinates, form contents or report contents to this analytics code.

The code loads only after you select Allow analytics. Declining does not affect calculations or purchases. Change your choice using Analytics preferences in the footer. Withdrawal stops subsequent collection by this code and removes accessible GA cookies for this website; it does not automatically delete previously processed data. Advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled. Declined consent, blockers and multiple devices affect measurement completeness.

Google may process data overseas. Read how Google uses partner-site data and its privacy policy.

Payments and other providers

Payments take place on Stripe, which processes payment and order information under its privacy policy. Existing visit counters use Busuanzi, and some pages load external fonts or resources. Such requests may send IP addresses and browser request information to those providers. The GA choice above controls the new GA4 integration, not existing third-party resources.

Personal analysis and contact

If you submit information by email, the recipient receives the contents you choose to send. For questions about these practices or your data, contact songjueqing@gmail.com.
